## The entry

> Look, the map said shortcut and I trust the map more than I trust you — you still think north is ‘that way’. The cul-de-sac has lovely roses. You’re welcome.

## Character count

157 characters — inside the 1–200 character gate.

## Why this line works

The GPS never apologises; it doubles down. It trusts the map more than the driver, and the driver's sense of direction is the punchline's setup — north as "that way" is the kind of thing only a lost person says. The cul-de-sac roses are the fresh, specific detail the judge brief wants instead of another "recalculating" joke, and "You're welcome" closes it with the GPS taking credit for the wrong turn. Smug, defensive, caught red-handed, blaming the map.
